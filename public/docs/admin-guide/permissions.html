Roles & Permissions

VK Finance ships with two roles:

RoleCan do
AdministratorEverything — manage users, settings, categories, exchange rates; view every family member's transactions and reports; view audit logs
MemberAdd/edit/delete their own transactions (if allowed in Settings); view their own dashboard and reports

Permissions are assigned by role, not hardcoded per page — this keeps the system ready to support more roles in the future without a code change, even though only Administrator and Member are available today.
