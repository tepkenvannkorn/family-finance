Transactions

Adding a transaction

  1. Go to Transactions and click + Add transaction.
  2. Choose Income or Expense.
  3. Enter the amount and choose the currency (KHR or USD) it was actually paid/received in.
  4. Pick a category — the list changes depending on whether you chose Income or Expense.
  5. Add a short description, and optionally notes and a receipt/invoice/bill (JPG, PNG, or PDF).
  6. Click Add transaction.

Editing or deleting

You can always edit or delete transactions you created yourself (unless your administrator has turned this off in Settings). Administrators can edit or delete any family member's transactions.

Searching and filtering

The Transactions page updates live as you type or change a filter — no page reload needed. You can filter by keyword, date range, type, currency, category, and amount range, and sort by clicking a column.

Attachments

Open a transaction and scroll down to see its attached files. Only people who could already see that transaction can open its attachments — links aren't publicly shareable.
