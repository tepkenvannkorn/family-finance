Reports

Go to Reports to see totals and a full transaction list for a period.

Choosing a period

Pick Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, or Custom range from the dropdown. You can further narrow by type or category.

Exporting

  • Export CSV — opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.
  • Export Excel — a formatted .xlsx spreadsheet.
  • Export PDF — a clean, printable document.
  • Print — prints the on-screen report directly (filters and buttons are hidden automatically).
