Logging In

VK Finance is a private application — only your family's administrator can create your account. You cannot sign up for yourself.

Steps

  1. Go to the app's web address. You'll land on the sign-in page.
  2. Enter the email and password your administrator gave you.
  3. Optionally check Remember me to stay signed in on this device for longer.
  4. Click Sign in.

Forgot your password?

Ask your administrator to reset it from the Users page. If self-service password reset is enabled, you can also change your own password anytime from My Profile once logged in.

Account locked?

After several incorrect password attempts, your account locks temporarily for security. Wait a few minutes and try again, or ask your administrator to unlock it immediately from the Users page.

VK Finance never asks you for your password by email or chat message. Only enter it on the app's own sign-in page.
