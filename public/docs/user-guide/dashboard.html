Dashboard

The dashboard is what you see right after logging in — a live snapshot of your family's finances.

Balance cards

  • Total income and total expense for the period, in your chosen display currency.
  • Balance — shown in green when positive, red when negative.

Charts

Below the balance cards you'll find: Income vs Expense, Monthly Trend, Weekly Trend, Expense Categories (a breakdown of this month's spending), and Currency Breakdown (how much of your activity is in KHR vs USD). A Recent Transactions list rounds out the default layout.

Customizing your layout

Click Customize layout to enter edit mode. You can then:

  • Drag a widget by its ⠿ handle to reorder it.
  • Resize to toggle a widget between a normal and a wide (full-row) size.
  • Hide a widget you don't need — hidden widgets appear as small "+" buttons at the bottom so you can bring them back anytime.

Your layout is saved automatically and is personal to you — other family members' dashboards aren't affected.

Display currency

Use the currency dropdown to view your totals as original (each transaction in the currency it was recorded in, summed separately), USD only, or KHR only. This only changes what you see — it never changes how a transaction was actually recorded.
