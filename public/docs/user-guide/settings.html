My Profile & Preferences

Click Profile in the top navigation to manage your own account.

Updating your details

Change your display name or email address anytime.

Changing your password

Enter your current password, then your new one twice. Your new password must meet the length/complexity rules your administrator has configured — the page will tell you if it doesn't.

If self-service password changes have been turned off by your administrator, ask them to reset it for you instead.
