VK Finance

A private, self-hosted web application for tracking your family's daily income and expenses in both Cambodian Riel (KHR) and US Dollar (USD), with a shared dashboard, reports, and per-family-member accounts.

New here? Start with Logging In, then Understanding the Dashboard. Setting up a fresh install? Go to Installation.

What this app does

  • Record income and expense transactions in KHR or USD, with categories, notes, and optional receipt/invoice attachments.
  • See a live dashboard: total income, total expense, balance, and charts for trends and category breakdowns.
  • Generate and export reports (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly/custom) as CSV, Excel, or PDF.
  • Manage family member accounts and roles (Administrator / Member) with an audit trail of important actions.
